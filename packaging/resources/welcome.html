BrewVerb

An open-source convolution reverb and space designer by Karun Prasad / Sound Brewery Studio.

This installer puts BrewVerb in the standard plug-in folders:

  • AAX → /Library/Application Support/Avid/Audio/Plug-Ins
  • AU → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • VST3 → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • VST → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST (when included in this build)
  • Sound Brewery IR Library → /Library/Application Support/Sound Brewery/BrewVerb/IR Library
    51 impulse responses from 36 spaces, each with a picture of the space, shown in BrewVerb's space browser.

Click Customize on the Installation Type screen to choose which formats (and the IR Library) to install. Restart your DAW after installing.
